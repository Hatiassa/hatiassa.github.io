Tinker Towers

A tower defense game: merge your towers mid-wave and defend your house across 30 levels.

An independent game made by one person

Try it in three steps

The game isn’t public yet: it’s in closed testing on Google Play, on Android phones only.

Coming from TikTok, Instagram or YouTube? Open this page in Chrome first, so you can sign in to Google.

  1. Join the group

    Tap Join group, then come back here. If Google says you don’t have permission, tap Sign in at the top of the page first.

    Open the group

  2. Become a tester

    On Google’s page, tap the button to become a tester, then come back here.

    Open the test page

  3. Install the game

    This button works once steps 1 and 2 are done. If Google Play can’t find the game, wait a little, check that it’s using the same Google account, then try again.

    Open Google Play

What you need

  • An Android phone
  • A Google account, the same one for all three steps
  • An internet connection, also needed to play

The game is free, with ads and optional purchases. It’s in English and French. Only the group’s managers can see its members.
