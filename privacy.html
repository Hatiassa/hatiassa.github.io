Tinker Towers privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

hatiassa ("we") publishes the game Tinker Towers for Android. Contact: tinkertowers.game@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores, on your device only

Your progress (levels won, stars, unlocked fusions, tips seen) and your settings (sound, language) are stored only on your device, in the app's storage. We do not receive them. They are deleted if you uninstall the game or clear its data. If Android's automatic backup is turned on for your device, Android may keep a copy in your Google account, according to your Android settings.

The game does not ask for an account, a name or an email address. It contains no analytics or crash reporting of our own.

2. Save export

If you choose "Export" in the settings, the game creates a file with your progress that you save or send yourself. We do not receive it. "Import" reads a file you choose, only on your device.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: ads between games (from level 3, at most one every two games) and videos you choose to watch for a reward (starting coins, second chance when the house falls, chests, daily videos, roulette spins). To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and share, according to Google: the IP address (which can be used to estimate the general location of the device), app interactions (launches, taps, video views), diagnostic information and device identifiers (Android advertising ID, app set ID). This data is encrypted in transit according to Google. Google processes it under its own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

At each launch, when online, the game asks Google's consent tool for the state of your choice, and Google's libraries prepare their advertising services. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your choice is requested at launch, before any ad, by Google's consent tool; you can change it at any time in the game's settings ("Ad privacy options"). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings.

The game is not directed at children under 13.

4. Premium Pack and perks (Google Play)

The Premium Pack (no ads between games, +50 starting coins and second chance without a video, ×3 speed) is a one-time purchase through Google Play. The coins, the second chance and the speed can also be bought on their own; removing ads takes the Premium Pack. Neither the Premium Pack nor these perks remove the videos you choose to watch for chests, daily videos and the roulette.

Payment is handled entirely by Google: we receive neither your payment details nor your address. The app receives from Google Play the receipt for your purchase (order number, technical token), nothing about your means of payment, and keeps on your device only the information that the item is owned. Google's policies apply to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

Notifications are optional: the game asks for your agreement, and you can turn them off at any time in the game's settings or in Android's settings. If you accept them, the game prepares at most two reminders a day on your device (full piggy bank, daily missions), which Android shows at the planned time. These reminders stay on your device: nothing is sent, neither to us nor to any other service.

6. Internet connection

An internet connection is required to play, even with the Premium Pack. To check it, the game sends a simple empty request to two Google servers (gstatic.com and google.com): at launch, at each level, when you come back to the game, when the network changes, and every 5 seconds while the game is waiting for a connection. This request contains no game data. Like any connection, it lets Google see the device's IP address.

7. Closed test, messages and this website

If you join the test group on Google Groups, its managers can see your Google account address. If you write to us by email or send a private comment from Google Play, we receive your message and the name or address that comes with it. We use them only to run the test and to reply to you. To stop, leave the group. To have your messages deleted, write to us.

This website is hosted by GitHub, which records visitors' IP addresses for security. The site itself uses no cookies and no analytics.

8. Your rights

Outside the test group and the messages you send us, we hold no personal data about you. For data processed by Google for ads and payments, see Google's policies above. Questions: tinkertowers.game@gmail.com.

9. Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted here, with its date.
